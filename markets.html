Short-term vs long-term. Each card shows two independent consensus reads: short-term (1h/4h/daily indicators — drives the alerts) and long-term (weekly/monthly indicators — the slow trend). When they disagree — e.g. long-term uptrend but short-term bearish — that is a pullback within a trend or the start of a reversal, and no indicator can tell you which. Neither timeframe is a prediction or a suggestion; both describe what already happened at different speeds.
What a "signal" is and is not. These alerts report when short-term technical indicators agree with each other. Indicator agreement describes the trend that already happened; it does not predict the next move, and it reverses without warning. Roughly half of consensus flips are followed by a move the other way. Use signals as a prompt to look, never as an instruction to trade.
